Carrier Onboarding Requirements

To haul loads for Black Roze Logistics, carriers must meet basic safety and compliance standards. Having your paperwork ready ensures quick setup:

  • Active MC/FF Number and USDOT Registration with active authority.
  • Certificate of Insurance (COI) displaying at least $100,000 in Cargo Liability and $1,000,000 in Auto Liability.
  • Signed W-9 Form.

Want to get set up in our system ahead of time? Reach out to our operations desk to receive our digital onboarding link.
