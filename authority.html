Regulatory standards, carrier vetting protocols, and risk management.

Federal Broker Registration Status

Black Roze Logistics LLC is registered in the State of Florida and is completing federal FMCSA Property Broker licensing (MC/USDOT), BMC-84 surety bond filing, and BOC-3 process agent registration ahead of full commercial operations.

Once issued, our live FMCSA MC Number, USDOT Number, and surety verification details will be published directly on this page and available upon request for shipper onboarding.

Carrier Risk & Insurance Standards

We enforce strict compliance controls before tendering any freight to third-party motor carriers:

  • FMCSA Verification: Active Operating Authority and Satisfactory (or clean Unrated) SAFER safety record.
  • Direct COI Verification: Minimum $100,000 Motor Truck Cargo and $1,000,000 Auto Liability sent directly from the insurance producer naming Black Roze Logistics LLC as Certificate Holder.
  • Anti-Fraud & Double-Brokering Controls: Strict identity, VIN/authority cross-checking, and zero tolerance for unauthorized re-brokering.
  • Port Credentials: Active UIIA interchange status and valid driver TWIC cards for all port drayage movements.
