Privacy Policy

Effective Date: January 1, 2027 | Black Roze Logistics LLC

1. Information We Collect

Black Roze Logistics LLC collects business contact and operational information submitted voluntarily through freight quote inquiries, carrier onboarding packets, and direct email communications—including names, company details, email addresses, phone numbers, shipment lane data, and motor carrier compliance records (MC/USDOT numbers, W-9 forms, and Certificates of Insurance).

2. How We Use Information

We use collected information strictly to provide freight rate quotes, verify motor carrier authority and insurance compliance, dispatch and track commercial shipments, process carrier settlements, and maintain regulatory records required by federal transportation law.

3. Information Sharing & Security

We do not sell, rent, or trade shipper or carrier information to third-party marketers. Operational shipment details are shared only with vetted motor carriers, shippers, receivers, or compliance verification partners as necessary to execute contracted freight movements.

4. Contact Us

For questions regarding data handling or to update your company records, contact dispatch@blackrozelogistics.com.
