About Black Roze Logistics

Black Roze Logistics is a freight brokerage built on discipline, transparency, and dependable execution. Headquartered in Jacksonville, Florida, we connect commercial shippers with vetted motor carriers across the Southeast and nationwide.

Our background spans vessel agency operations, international import logistics, and personnel/fleet coordination — giving us firsthand insight into how costly port delays, missed appointments, and unvetted capacity can be. We apply that same maritime-grade precision to every load we manage.
