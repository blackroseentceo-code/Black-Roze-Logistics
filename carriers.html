Carrier Onboarding Requirements

To haul freight for Black Roze Logistics, carriers must meet strict safety, authority, and compliance standards. Having your documentation ready ensures fast setup:

1. Operating Authority & Safety

  • Active Authority: Active FMCSA MC/FF Number and USDOT Registration (minimum 6 months active operating authority preferred).
  • Safety Rating: Satisfactory or Unrated FMCSA safety rating with clean SAFER inspection history (no Conditional or Unsatisfactory ratings).
  • Port Drayage (If Applicable): Active UIIA interchange status and valid driver TWIC credentials for port and rail terminal access.

2. Insurance & Documentation

  • Certificate of Insurance (COI): Minimum $100,000 Motor Truck Cargo and $1,000,000 Auto Liability, sent directly from your insurance producer with Black Roze Logistics LLC listed as Certificate Holder.
  • Tax & Payment Setup: Completed and signed W-9 Form, plus ACH Direct Deposit authorization or Factoring Notice of Assignment (NOA).
  • Carrier Agreement: Executed Black Roze Logistics Broker-Carrier Agreement prior to first dispatch.
