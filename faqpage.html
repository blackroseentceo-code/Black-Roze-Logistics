Frequently Asked Questions

Answers for shippers and motor carriers partnering with Black Roze Logistics.

What types of freight does Black Roze Logistics handle?

We specialize in Dry Van Truckload (TL), Port Drayage & Intermodal container moves (including JAXPORT terminals), Regional Dedicated Loops, and Healthcare & Medical non-hazardous freight.

How do you vet motor carriers before tendering a load?

Every carrier undergoes authority, safety, and insurance verification. We require active FMCSA operating authority, a Satisfactory or clean Unrated safety profile, an executed Broker-Carrier Agreement, and a Certificate of Insurance ($100,000 Cargo / $1,000,000 Auto Liability) sent directly from the insurance producer with Black Roze Logistics LLC listed as Certificate Holder.

Do you coordinate port drayage at JAXPORT?

Yes. We manage container drayage between JAXPORT terminals and regional distribution centers, working exclusively with UIIA-interchange approved carriers and TWIC-credentialed drivers.

How do carriers get onboarded and paid?

Carriers can email dispatch@blackrozelogistics.com with their MC/DOT number to receive our digital onboarding packet. Once your W-9, COI, Broker-Carrier Agreement, and ACH or Factoring Notice of Assignment (NOA) are approved, you are cleared for dispatch.

How quickly can I receive a freight quote?

Submit your origin, destination, equipment type, and shipment details on our Quote page, and our operations desk will respond promptly with current lane pricing.
