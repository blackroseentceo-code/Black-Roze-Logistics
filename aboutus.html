About Black Roze Logistics

Precision freight brokerage rooted in maritime, port, and regional supply chain operations.

Black Roze Logistics is a freight brokerage built on discipline, transparency, and dependable execution. Headquartered in Jacksonville, Florida, we connect commercial shippers with vetted motor carriers across the Southeast and nationwide.

Who We Are

Headquartered in Jacksonville, Florida, Black Roze Logistics LLC is a non-asset freight brokerage built on operational discipline, transparent communication, and strict regulatory compliance. Strategically positioned near one of the Southeast's premier deep-water ports (JAXPORT) and major interstate corridors (I-95 and I-10), we connect commercial shippers with vetted, dependable motor carriers.

Our background spans vessel agency operations, international import logistics, and personnel/fleet coordination — giving us firsthand insight into how costly port delays, missed appointments, and unvetted capacity can be. We apply that same maritime-grade precision to every dry van, drayage, regional dedicated loop, and healthcare freight movement we manage.

Our Mission

Our mission is simple: move freight with precision and build lasting partnerships based on trust. We believe that clear communication, honest pricing, and consistent performance are the foundation of every successful shipment.

Our Approach

We take a disciplined, process-driven approach to every load. From initial quote to final delivery, our team focuses on the details — verifying carrier credentials, monitoring shipments in real time, and communicating proactively with both shippers and carriers.

Why Choose Black Roze

  • Jacksonville-based with deep knowledge of JAXPORT and Southeast freight lanes
  • Vetted carrier network with strict safety and compliance standards
  • Transparent pricing with no hidden fees
  • Proactive communication throughout every shipment
  • Dedicated support from quote to delivery

Our Operating Standard

We believe brokerage should eliminate friction, not add to it. Rather than relying on bloated call centers or unverified load-board speculation, we build repeatable regional lanes with thoroughly screened carriers — verifying active FMCSA authority, safety records, TWIC/UIIA port credentials, and direct certificate-holder insurance before a single load is dispatched.

  • Every carrier is vetted for safety, insurance, and authority before dispatch
  • Every load is tracked and monitored from pickup to delivery
  • Every shipper receives clear, honest communication at every step
