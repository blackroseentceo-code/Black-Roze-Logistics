Frequently Asked Questions

For Shippers: How do I track active freight?

Once a load is dispatched, you will receive real-time visibility milestone updates and direct status lines from our operations management team.

For Shippers: What equipment categories do you support?

We manage nationwide networks encompassing Dry Vans, Reefers, Flatbeds, and specialized partial/LTL shipping configurations.

For Carriers: What is required for onboarding approval?

Motor carriers must provide active MC/DOT authority verification, a completed W-9 form, and a valid certificate of insurance meeting our standard cargo and auto liability minimums.

For Carriers: Do you provide quick pay structures?

Yes, quick pay options are available upon clean document submission, including signed bills of lading (BOLs) and rate confirmations.
